# launch — Launch: warmed audience, sequenced channels, and launch-day metrics.

## Intake

- What is launching and its target audience
- Positioning and key message (from positioning mode)
- Channels and existing audience to warm
- Launch date, budget, and success metrics
- Default stack: Kutt short links for campaign URLs where available, else any shortener or full URLs.

## Deliverable

A launch plan: pre-launch/warm-up, launch-day, and post-launch phases with channel sequencing, asset list, owners, timeline, and success metrics per phase.

## Procedure

1. Define the launch goal and the metric that signals success.
2. Warm the audience before the ask: teasers, early access, waitlist, community seeding.
3. Choose and sequence channels per phase (email, social, PR, paid, partners).
4. Build the asset list: landing page, emails, posts, demo, FAQ — routed to `content`/`design`.
5. Set the timeline with owners and dependencies (day −14 → day 0 → day +14).
6. Prepare launch-day operations: monitoring, support, war-room for issues.
7. Plan post-launch follow-up: re-engage non-converters, collect feedback, measure and iterate.
8. Stress-test the plan before committing: argue against it (devil's advocate), sweep blind spots (confirmation bias, underestimated complexity, ICP drift, channel saturation, sequencing error, resource mismatch, timing miss), ask the one uncomfortable question, then ship the minimum viable version first.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Launch goal and success metric defined.
- [ ] Audience warmed before the ask.
- [ ] Channels sequenced per phase.
- [ ] ORB coverage: every phase draws on owned, rented, AND borrowed channels — a single-type plan is incomplete.
- [ ] Asset list with owners and dependencies.
- [ ] Post-launch follow-up and measurement planned.
- [ ] Webinars run as funnels: reg → show → engage → convert targets set, a show-up sequence scheduled, segmented attendee vs no-show follow-up planned.
- [ ] Plan survived devil's-advocate + blind-spot sweep; MVP version defined first.
- [ ] Pre-build risk verdict recorded: demand/positioning/monetization/retention/trust/distribution scored, main assumption + smallest validation named (keeper: wshobson/before-you-build).

## Routing

- Extended adversarial review → secretary socratic-gate mode; asset production → content/design modes.
